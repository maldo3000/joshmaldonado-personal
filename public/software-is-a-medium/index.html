The pattern

Every medium got cheaper to make

Every new technology for making things started out locked behind a skill, a machine or a gatekeeper. Then it got cheap, and the people who had only been watching started to make things too.

Each time, the hard part moved. It stopped being about whether you could produce the thing at all, and became about whether you had anything to put in it. Here is that story from cave walls to software, nine times over. Keep scrolling and each scene builds itself.
